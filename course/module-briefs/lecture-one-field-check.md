## Interaction rationale

- AI101 family: retrieval and construction or diagnosis
- Components: `FlashcardDeck` and `CategoryChallenge`
- Learner action: recall, self-rate, then classify fresh cases
- Conceptual target: distinguish signal and latency rules across contexts
- Visible response: revealed answer, self-rating, selected category, and persistent feedback
- Completion: every card rated and every case attempted
- Revisit and reset: stable IDs, versioned persistence, back and reset controls
- State: card reveal and rating plus case choices; malformed state recovers to fresh
- Transfer: utility, retail, finance, routing, and fraud cases differ from the teaching examples

## Assessment and feedback

Formative only. Self-rating is described as a study signal, not a grade. Wrong classifications explain the controlling clue.
